# Small-Business Benchmarks: Trading Tools, Betting Tools and Market-Data Niches (as of Oct 2026)

Evidence labels used throughout: **[Verified]** = regulatory filing / SEC / official acquisition release; **[Strong]** = reputable press citing filings or named sources; **[Anecdotal]** = company self-report, careers page, third-party estimate (Datanyze/Latka-type), review site; **[Speculative]** = my inference; **[Unknown]** = searched, nothing reliable found.

Method note: ~23 web searches, 2 fetches (both fetches failed on DNS — tradezella.com and betakit.com — so those items rely on search snippets only). Companies House was NOT directly queried (no access in-session); UK firm financials (Bet Angel, OddsMonkey/Outplayed) remain gaps. Self-reported revenue is treated sceptically throughout.

## Q1. Benchmark set: who, how big, how fast, which channel, what price, what outcome

### Takeaway
Of ~24 businesses checked, only a handful have hard numbers. The best-documented "small team, big result" cases are OddsJam (self-funded to ~$26M revenue / $12M EBITDA, sold for $80M + up to $80M earnout), Darwinex (FCA-regulated, ~€7M revenue, thin profit), Quiver Quantitative (student founders, >$2M ARR profitable), RebelBetting (4 staff, ~SEK6.7M revenue at ~54% EBIT margin, dated) and Card Ladder (founded June 2020, acquired ~18 months later). Most trading-journal businesses (TradeZella, Edgewonk, TraderSync, Tradervue) are durable but opaque.

### Inferences
- [Speculative] A realistic "very successful small business" band in this space is **$1M–$10M revenue with 1–25 staff**: RebelBetting (~$0.6–0.7M revenue, 4 staff, ~54% EBIT margin at the time), Quiver (>$2M ARR), TrendSpider (≥$2M by 2021), Edgewonk (~$5.5M est.), Darwinex (€7M but regulated and thin-margin). - [Speculative] Darwinex vs RebelBetting is the instructive contrast: the regulated model earned ~8% net margin on €7M; the unregulated 4-person software tool earned ~54% EBIT margin. Licences add revenue ceilings but crush margin for a solo founder.

## Q2. Revenue levels and speed for small teams

### Takeaway
The fastest documented trajectories (Card Ladder ~18 months to exit; Quiver to >$2M ARR within a few years; Koinly to ~110 staff within ~3 years; OddsJam >5x YoY) all rode a **market mania** (2020–21 retail/cards/crypto, 2021+ US sports-betting legalisation). Steadier tools (Darwinex, journals, RebelBetting) took ~5–15 years to reach low-to-mid single-digit millions.

### Cited Findings
- Card Ladder began operations June 2020; acquired by Collectors Universe Dec 2021 — [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/business/commerce/2021/collectors-universe-buys-card-ladder-1234647703/) [Verified]
- Quiver: founded Feb 2020 by students; later >$2M ARR, profitable — [In Business Madison](https://www.ibmadison.com/industries/technology-biotech/doubling-down-on-data/article_d5bb1509-7b2d-449b-b4be-de18d05687e8.html) [Strong; self-reported revenue]
- Koinly: 3-month prototype, launched 2019; 15x users YoY and ~110 staff by 2022 — [CFOtech](https://cfotech.co.uk/story/exclusive-koinly-makes-it-easy-to-invest-in-the-crypto-market); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/koinly) [Strong/Anecdotal]
- Collectr: five-figure ARR "for most of its lifetime" before inflecting to "eight-figure" — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/how-collectr-bootstrapped-a-trading-card-hobby-into-an-eight-figure-business/) [Strong; note "eight-figure" may refer to revenue run-rate or valuation — snippet ambiguous]
- Darwinex: €2.7M (FY2019) to €7.0M (2023) — [FNG](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/darwinex-operator-tradeslide-sees-10-revenue-jump-in-2023-to-e7-0m) [Strong]
- TrendSpider: Inc. 5000 #130 in 2022 (3-yr growth ranking, ≥$2M 2021 revenue floor) — [TrendSpider](https://trendspider.com/blog/trendspider-announcement-trendspider-ranks-no-130-on-the-2022-inc-5000-annual-list/) [Strong]

### Inferences
- [Speculative] Collectr's "five-figure ARR for most of its lifetime" is the most realistic base case for a solo founder: long flat period, then inflection when a channel or monetisation model clicks.
- [Speculative] Timing to a demand wave matters more than product polish; a pre-trade check tool has no obvious mania tailwind, implying slower, steadier growth (journal-like) rather than a Card Ladder/OddsJam curve.

### Gaps
- No reliable month-by-month MRR data for any trading journal. No Indie Hackers/Starter Story revenue interviews surfaced in the searches for these specific companies.

## Q3. Acquisition channels that worked

### Takeaway

### Gaps

## Q4. Which needed regulatory licences

### Takeaway
Pure software/data/journal tools (OddsJam, RebelBetting, Bet Angel, journals, Quiver, Card Ladder, Collectr) operated without financial-services licences. Anything that **holds money, places trades, or manages assets** needed licences or a licensed partner: Darwinex (FCA broker/asset manager), Autopilot and Composer (US copy/automated investing, Composer via Alpaca broker API), Stocktwits' brokerage arm (later sold).

### Cited Findings
- Darwinex: FCA-regulated (FRN 586466) multi-asset broker and asset manager; sought Spanish CNMV licence for EU post-Brexit — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/brokers/darwinex-fy2020-revenue-jumps-72-seeking-spanish-license/amp/) [Strong]
- Composer built on Alpaca Broker API, US-only — [Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/blog/composer-partners-with-alpaca-broker-api); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/breather-ritual-alumni-look-to-ride-retail-investing-wave-with-new-automated-trading-platform-composer/) [Strong]
- Stocktwits sold its ~15,000-account brokerage (TradeApp) to Public for < $10M and refocused on community — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/pro/fintech-deals/2024/04/16/public-acquires-stocktwits-trading-accounts) [Strong]
- Matched betting (UK) is unlicensed but subject to ASA advertising rules; OddsMonkey received ASA rulings for misleading ads — [ASA ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/dotnetpages-ltd-a18-474571.html); [Lewis Silkin](https://adlaw.lewissilkin.com/post/102ev65/not-such-a-safe-bet-asa-rules-on-misleading-gambling-ads) [Verified]

### Inferences
- [Speculative] For a UK solo founder, Veto as a check/ledger tool (no execution, no advice on specific trades) likely stays outside FCA perimeter, but any feature that "tells users what to trade" or auto-executes risks investment-advice / arranging-deals perimeter questions. This needs a separate regulatory check.
- [Speculative] Stocktwits' sale of its brokerage suggests even well-funded tool/community companies find licensed activities a poor fit.

### Gaps
- Whether Autopilot is an SEC-registered investment adviser was not confirmed in sources found. FCA perimeter analysis for UK signal/copy services was not researched here.

## Q5. What killed or stunted businesses

### Takeaway
Documented failure modes: **trust/security breach** (3Commas API keys), **incumbent IP/legal attack** (Entain vs OddsMonkey/Outplayed), **advertising regulation** (ASA vs OddsMonkey), **forced consolidation in a mature niche** (OddsMonkey + Outplayed merger), **dependency on a single data source/personality** (Autopilot's Pelosi portfolio ends with her retirement), **cost growth eating margin in regulated models** (Darwinex profit fell 2020–21 despite revenue growth). Direct evidence of "performance marketing collapsing in drawdowns" was not found.

### Cited Findings
- 3Commas: first complaints Oct 2022, company initially denied problems, CEO confirmed leak 29 Dec 2022 after CZ warning; HAPI verified $27.3M losses across 86 users from 32 countries as of 10 Jan 2023, estimating thousands affected; attacker claimed ~100k keys and published 10k; exchanges (Binance) refused reimbursement — [ForkLog/HAPI](https://forklog.com/en/news/api-key-leaks-and-exchange-inaction-a-hapi-analysis-of-the-3commas-incident); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/3commas-ceo-confirms-api-key-leak-following-warning-from-cz) [Strong]
- FBI reportedly investigating 3Commas (unnamed sources) and class action reportedly prepared — [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/the-fbi-is-reportedly-probing-3commas-after-api-keys-leaked-on-twitter) [Anecdotal]
- Entain sued OddsMonkey/Outplayed holding companies (Liquidity Trading Ltd, Liquidity PA Ltd) in English High Court for passing off and trade-mark infringement; outcome not found — [iGaming Next](https://next.io/?p=135928); [AffPapa](https://affpapa.com/entain-targets-oddsmonkey-in-a-new-intellectual-property-case/) [Strong]
- Autopilot will stop tracking Pelosi on her retirement, shifting to a "general insider trading" portfolio — [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/autopilot-raises-8m-expands-operations) [Strong]
- Darwinex profit €683K (FY19) → €470K (FY20) → €225K (FY21) despite revenue doubling — [FNG](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/exclusive-darwinex-revenues-up-12-in-2021-to-e5-3-million/); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/fintech/darwinex-posts-growth-in-revenue-profits-decline/) [Strong]
- RebelBetting user review: barely broke even after subscription costs — [Trustpilot](https://at.trustpilot.com/review/rebelbetting.com) [Anecdotal]
- DCG closed TradeBlock trading unit in 2023 citing crypto winter and regulatory uncertainty — [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/cryptocurrencies/news/first-mover-americas-digital-currency-group-is-closing-down-tradeblock-202305261257) [Strong]

### Inferences
- [Speculative] Bookmaker account restrictions ("gubbing") are the well-known ceiling on value/arb/matched-betting tools; users churn once limited. Not sourced in this session, but consistent with OddsJam pivoting value into B2B data (OpticOdds).
- [Speculative] The 3Commas case is the strongest argument for Veto **not** holding trade-enabled API keys: read-only or manual-input designs avoid the single largest documented tail risk in this category.
- [Speculative] Tools whose value is "you'll profit" face the user's own P&L as a churn trigger (RebelBetting review); tools priced against "avoid one bad trade" may churn less, but there is no data here to prove it.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on churn spikes in signal/bot businesses during drawdowns (e.g., 2022 crypto bear). No outcome of Entain v OddsMonkey. 3Commas' current revenue/state unknown.

## Q6. Do "lose less / discipline" tools sell, or only "more profit" tools? Retention evidence

### Takeaway
Discipline tools clearly *sell*: trading journals are a crowded category of long-lived, profitable, mostly bootstrapped firms (Tradervue since 2011, TraderSync since 2014, Edgewonk ~$5.5M est., TradeZella "highly profitable"). But they are marketed as a **path to profitability**, not as loss-avoidance. The largest outcomes (OddsJam, matched betting, copy-trading) sell profit directly. **No hard retention data** was found for journals vs signals vs bots.

### Cited Findings
- TradeZella frames its mission as helping "1 million retail day traders reach consistent profitability"; describes itself as bootstrapped and highly profitable — [TradeZella careers](https://www.tradezella.com/careers) [Anecdotal]
- Tradervue has served traders since 2011 — [Tradervue](https://www.tradervue.com/site/edgewonk-alternative) [Anecdotal]
- TraderSync founded 2014 — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/tradersync) [Anecdotal]
- Edgewonk ~$5.5M revenue, 24 employees (estimate) — [Datanyze](https://www.datanyze.com/companies/edgewonk/415056718) [Anecdotal]
- Multiple journal alternatives sites exist (crowded market) — [daytradingz](https://daytradingz.com/tradezella-alternatives/); [Traders Second Brain](https://traderssecondbrain.com/guides/edgewonk-alternative) [Anecdotal]
- Profit-promise tools: RebelBetting markets turning betting "into an investment"; matched betting markets risk-free profit and was censured by ASA — [Trustpilot/RebelBetting](https://at.trustpilot.com/review/rebelbetting.com); [ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/dotnetpages-ltd-a18-474571.html) [Anecdotal/Verified]

### Inferences
- [Speculative] Journal longevity (10–15 years for several players) is indirect evidence of decent retention among the subset of serious traders; journals likely retain better than signals because their value doesn't depend on a strategy keeping working.
- [Speculative] Positioning lesson for Veto: frame as "protect your edge / stop rule-breaking trades" with a profitability narrative, rather than pure loss-prevention; a journal-adjacent ledger is a proven-sellable category, but a pre-trade gate is a newer behaviour with no direct comparables found.
- [Speculative] Market is crowded and commoditising at the journal layer ($15–80/mo); differentiation would need to come from the pre-trade check or AI analysis.

### Gaps
- No public churn/retention figures for any journal, signal service, or bot found. No case found of a business explicitly marketed as "lose less" reaching scale.

## Q7. Exit landscape: acquirers and multiples

### Takeaway
Acquirers are **vertical consolidators and adjacent platforms**, not big banks: gambling affiliates buying data/tools (Gambling.com → OddsJam), collectibles grading monopolies (Collectors → Card Ladder, Beckett), crypto VCs (Lightspeed Crypto → Coinigy), brokerages buying accounts (Public → Stocktwits TradeApp), communities buying AI (Stocktwits → Thematic). The only disclosed multiple: OddsJam ~6.7x 2024 adj. EBITDA / ~3.1x revenue upfront, up to ~13.3x EBITDA / ~6.2x revenue with full earnout.

### Cited Findings
- Gambling.com: $80M upfront + up to $80M earnout for Odds Holdings, earnout on 2025–26 adj. EBITDA, up to 50% payable in shares; funded by Wells Fargo facility — [SEC 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000183979925000022/gamb-20241231.htm) [Verified]
- Press: earnout requires adj. EBITDA to at least double in 2026 vs 2024 — [SBC Americas](https://sbcamericas.com/2024/12/12/gamblingcom-buying-oddsjam-parent/) [Strong; conflicts in detail with filing wording]
- Collectors Universe acquired Card Ladder (terms undisclosed); later Collectors acquired Beckett (Dec 2025) — [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/business/commerce/2021/collectors-universe-buys-card-ladder-1234647703/); [PrivSource](https://www.privsource.com/acquisitions/deal/deSq6n) [Verified]
- Lightspeed Crypto acquired Coinigy (Aug 2021, terms undisclosed) — [Lightspeed](https://lightspeed.com/news/lightspeed-crypto-acquires-coinigy-one-of-the-industrys-leading-crypto-trading-platforms) [Verified]
- Public bought ~15,000 Stocktwits brokerage accounts for < $10M — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/pro/fintech-deals/2024/04/16/public-acquires-stocktwits-trading-accounts) [Strong]
- Stocktwits acquired Thematic (Jul 2025) — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/stocktwits-buying-thematic-for-ai-based-research-platform/amp/) [Strong]

### Inferences
- [Speculative] OddsJam multiple computed from seller-projected 2024 figures ($26M rev / $12M EBITDA): $80M / $12M ≈ 6.7x; $160M / $12M ≈ 13.3x. The B2B data API (OpticOdds) likely drove the premium — the acquirer described itself afterwards as a "data and marketing company".
- [Speculative] For a solo-founder SaaS at $0.5–3M ARR, the realistic exit path is a micro-PE/marketplace sale (Acquire.com-type) or a strategic in the same vertical (broker, prop firm, journal competitor, betting affiliate); no such small-deal multiples were found in this research.
- [Speculative] B2B data (option d) appears to command higher exit value than B2C tools in this sample.

### Gaps
- No disclosed multiples for Card Ladder, Coinigy, or any trading journal. No Acquire.com / FE International trading-tool deal data found. Earnout actually paid by Gambling.com for 2025 not found.
