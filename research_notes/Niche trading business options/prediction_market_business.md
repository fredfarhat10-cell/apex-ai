## 1. Market size: volumes, users, growth, number of venues (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Volume grew roughly 10x in a year, and sports drives most of it. Combined Kalshi + Polymarket volume went from under $5B/month (Sep 2025) to a record of about $50.6B in July 2026, with Kalshi at roughly 3x Polymarket. The venue count has also jumped: brokers, sportsbooks, exchanges and crypto-native venues have all launched prediction products. More venues means more data fragmentation, which helps cross-venue tools. It also means incumbents with distribution are crowding in.

## 2. Ecosystem businesses and traction: who is making money

### Takeaway
The proven money is in three places. Execution layers that take a cut of order flow earn real revenue (Kreo ~$0.57M/yr; Based ~$1M/yr in builder rewards). Data licensing to media is captured by the venues themselves (CNBC, CNN, Fox, AP, Dow Jones). Institutional infrastructure is where VC money is going (River Markets $8.5M, Furnace $3.5M). Retail analytics and whale trackers are crowded and cheap ($9-50/mo, with strong free tiers). Unified APIs have already consolidated: Polymarket bought Dome.

## 3. Gaps: what traders say is missing

### Takeaway
I couldn't get direct Reddit or X complaint evidence because search returned no Reddit threads. Indirect evidence points to resolution risk, tax and P&L record-keeping, and verifying that two contracts are really the same as the recurring pain points. Arbitrage discovery is already well served.

### Cited Findings
- **[Strong]** Resolution processes differ by venue. Kalshi's internal team decides outcomes and there is no binding trader dispute ("Request to Settle" is only a suggestion). On Polymarket, anyone can dispute by posting a **$750 USDC bond**, which escalates to a token vote. Guides tell traders to screenshot ambiguous resolution announcements. — [DeFi Rate: how contracts settle](https://defirate.com/prediction-markets/how-contracts-settle/)
- **[Strong]** Similarly worded markets on different venues can settle differently. Verification tools exist because of this, and wording differences can erase arb edges. — [Apify verifier](https://apify.com/redfoxxie/polymarket-kalshi-arbitrage-verifier); [StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/arbitrage/kalshi)
- **[Anecdotal]** Polymarket issues no 1099s, so users must track every deposit, trade and withdrawal at USD value themselves, using spreadsheets or crypto tax software (Koinly, CoinTracker, TokenTax). Kalshi issues 1099-B above thresholds. Treatment is disputed: one source says Section 1256 60/40 may apply, another says ordinary income. — [Laika Labs](https://laikalabs.ai/prediction-markets/prediction-market-tax-records-tracking); [Camuso CPA](https://camusocpa.com/prediction-market-accounting-guide/); [AlphaScope](https://www.alphascope.app/blog/prediction-market-tax-guide); [PredictionMarketsReviews](https://predictionmarketsreviews.com/strategies/prediction-market-tax-guide)
- **[Anecdotal]** Vendors are already crowding into prediction-market tax content (PredictEngine, AlphaScope, Laika Labs, TradeAlgo). — [PredictEngine](https://www.predictengine.ai/blog/prediction-market-tax-reporting-5-methods-compared-for-august-2025); [TradeAlgo](https://www.tradealgo.com/trading-guides/prediction-markets/prediction-market-tax-guide)

### Inferences
- **[Speculative]** Candidate gaps ranked by fit for this founder:
  1. **Resolution-risk analytics**: score each market's rule wording, oracle and dispute history, and the chance a cross-venue "pair" settles differently. This is underserved and fits LLM agents well.
  2. **Cross-venue portfolio and risk** (exposure, correlation, scenario P&L) across Kalshi, Polymarket, Robinhood and the UK exchanges. River Markets is chasing the institutional end.
  3. **Calibration and edge tracking** (Brier scores, closing-line value by category) for serious traders and syndicates.
  4. **Tax/P&L export.** There's demand, but jurisdiction-specific US tax advice is a liability, and crypto-tax incumbents already import wallets.
- **[Speculative]** Consumer arb scanners and whale alerts are not gaps.

### Gaps
- **[Unknown]** Direct, quantified trader complaints from r/Polymarket, r/Kalshi and X. Searches returned no Reddit threads. The founder should scrape or read these subreddits directly before choosing a wedge.

## 4. Legal: UK company selling to non-UK traders; affiliates; UKGC/ASA; US state actions; ToS

### Takeaway
Selling read-only analytics software to non-UK traders looks to be the lowest-risk model, though I found no UK ruling that addresses it directly. Promoting or referring UK consumers to unlicensed venues, or routing orders for them, is the dangerous part. The Gambling Commission treats prediction markets as gambling (betting intermediary), and unlicensed operators must not target GB. US state litigation, mostly over sports contracts, is the biggest ecosystem-level risk, and a circuit split points toward the Supreme Court.

### Cited Findings
**UK position:**
- **[Strong]** UKGC, 4 Feb 2026 (blog by Brad Enright, Director of Strategy): current prediction-market products "would appear" to fall within the **"Betting Intermediary"** definition. They can't classify themselves as non-gambling. **Current unlicensed operators "should take steps to ensure they are not targeting or transacting with consumers in Great Britain"**, and there are criminal offences for operating without a licence. — [InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/igaming/news/prediction-markets-would-likely-be-classed-as-gambling-in-gb); [Gambling.com](https://www.gambling.com/uk/news/are-prediction-markets-legal-in-the-uk); [FocusGN](https://focusgn.com/gambling-commission-warns-prediction-markets-not-to-target-the-uk); [SiGMA](https://sigma.world/news/ukgc-prediction-markets-gambling-regulation/) (primary blog not opened)
- **[Strong]** FCA, 6 Oct 2026 annual public meeting: Simon Walls (Markets ED) said the FCA is "engaging" with Polymarket and Kalshi. This does **not** mean authorisation or a new framework. The FCA treats the financial prediction products it has examined as **binary options**, and the 2019 retail binary-options ban remains. Sports and political contracts fall under the UKGC; financial contracts under the FCA. — [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/crypto-web3-and-cbdc/news/fca-reportedly-confirms-talks-as-prediction-markets-push-back-on-gambling-label); [CCN](https://www.ccn.com/news/crypto/polymarket-kalshi-uk-fca-prediction-markets-push-back-gambling/); [Casino.com](https://www.casino.com/news/industry/fca-confirms-prediction-market-talks-as-kalshi-and-polymarket-eye-britain/). Some outlets framed this as the FCA "weighing lifting the retail ban" — [CoinMarketCap Academy](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/uk-fca-weighs-lifting-retail-ban-on-prediction-markets-like-polymarket); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/uk-fca-easing-prediction-market-ban/). Another says the FCA is "cooling" on prediction markets — [iGamingExpert](https://igamingexpert.com/regions/europe/fca-cooling-prediction-markets-uk-gambling/?amp=1). **Sources conflict on direction; no decision found after early Oct 2026.**
- **[Strong]** Polymarket is blocked or close-only in 41 jurisdictions including the UK. Kalshi lists the UK as restricted. UK users reportedly reach the platforms through VPNs. — [CryptoSlate review](https://cryptoslate.com/prediction-markets/polymarket-review/); [Casino.com](https://www.casino.com/news/industry/fca-confirms-prediction-market-talks-as-kalshi-and-polymarket-eye-britain/)
- **[Strong]** Dutch precedent: the Netherlands regulator took enforcement action against Polymarket's operator for letting Dutch residents participate without effective geoblocking. — [FocusGN summary](https://focusgn.com/gambling-commission-warns-prediction-markets-not-to-target-the-uk)

The UK's category could not be confirmed. ## 5. First-mover angle on UK venues

### Takeaway
UK prediction products already exist, but they run on existing betting-exchange licences: Matchbook Predictions / ADI Predictstreet, Betfair Predicts (invite-only beta), Smarkets. IG is targeting Q4 2026 but depends on a Gambling Commission licence. Volumes are small next to the US, APIs and data terms for the new products are unclear, and the FCA retail ban on financial binaries still stands. A "UK prediction-market toolkit" is a real first-mover niche, but a thin and timing-risky one. It is best built as a module on a venue-agnostic product.

### Cited Findings
- **[Strong]** IG Group confirmed a Q4 2026 launch of a UK prediction-markets arm after applying for a Gambling Commission licence. Some aspects will be FCA-regulated. No grant confirmed. — [InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/igaming/news/ig-group-uk-prediction-markets-q4)

### Inferences
- **[Speculative]** A plausible "UK prediction-market toolkit":
  1. a normalised data feed and price comparison across Matchbook Predictions, ADI Predictstreet, Betfair (exchange and Predicts), Smarkets, and IG when it's live, mapped to equivalent Kalshi/Polymarket contracts as read-only reference prices;
  2. cross-venue exposure and risk dashboards;
  3. resolution-rule comparison between UK exchange settlement rules and US venues;
  4. agent-driven alerts;
  5. for UK users only, links to **licensed** venues.
  Selling to UK users who trade on UK-licensed venues avoids the "unlicensed operator" problem completely.
- **[Speculative]** The existing UK exchange-automation market (Bet Angel, Geeks Toy and similar Betfair tools) shows UK traders will pay for exchange tooling. Prediction products on the same exchanges extend that market naturally, and those incumbents could add the feature quickly.
- **[Speculative] Risks of building ahead of venues:**
  - IG's licence may slip, and its retail sports product isn't due until 2027.
  - New venue APIs may not exist, or may forbid redistribution.
  - UK liquidity is small, so the paying audience is small.
  - The FCA may never open financial binaries to retail.
  - If Kalshi or Polymarket do get UK authorisation, they'll bring their own tools and builder ecosystems.
  Mitigation: build venue-agnostic and sell to non-UK pros now, and add UK venue adapters as each one goes live.
- **[Speculative]** Partnership or white-label is possibly the better route than retail: UK venues launching thin prediction books will need market data, reference pricing from US venues, and risk tooling. Licensed operators can also run affiliate programmes that UK affiliates can legally join, subject to UKGC and CAP rules.
