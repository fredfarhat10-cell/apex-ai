## 2. Lead-lag: weekend and overnight behaviour, Monday-open deviation, futures, oracles, NAV premium/discount, mint/redeem

### Takeaway
The evidence on whether weekend token prices pre-empt Monday's open is mixed and mostly vendor-funded: one study claims 92% capture, another claims about 10%. The structural facts are clear, though. Primary arbitrage (mint/redeem) is limited to KYC'd or approved institutions and mostly to US market hours. Market makers widen spreads on weekends because they cannot hedge. The "faster source of truth" over a weekend is therefore weak: CME equity-index futures (open Sunday evening ET) only cover indices and a few single names, and trade.xyz perps set their own weekend price with no outside reference. Any weekend "lag" is mostly a risk premium for gap risk, not a free stale quote.

### Cited Findings
- Binance Research (bStocks) studied 7 weekends. Token prices captured a median 92% of the Monday-open move, and the residual deviation at the open was 0.19%. (Anecdotal: vendor-sourced, conflict of interest, small sample.) — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/thought-leadership/the-convergence-trade-nobody-planned-on-chain-fridays-to-nyse-mondays/)
- An independent check of 13 weekends across 6 issuers found that by 6pm Sunday New York time, the typical token had covered only about 10% of the gap to its stock's Monday open. (Anecdotal: published on KuCoin's insight section; I could not read the methodology.) This contradicts the Binance figure. — [KuCoin Insight](https://www.kucoin.com/news/insight/AAPL/6ac4857938a264000792c769)
- Academic: Cong, Landsman, Rabetti, Zhang and Zhao, "Tokenized Stocks" (SSRN 5937314). Tokens track the underlying closely during regular hours and deviate modestly off-hours. Weekend moves anticipate the Monday open rather than overshoot it. (Strong evidence: peer-style working paper; read via abstract summary only.) — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5937314)
- World Federation of Exchanges study of 61 token-stock pairs: about 80% are cointegrated with the underlying. Traditional shares drive most price discovery, and tokens make the larger adjustment when the two diverge. The analysis is daily and excludes weekends. (Strong evidence: WFE.) — [EconoTimes](https://econotimes.com/Stock-and-ETF-Tokens-Make-Larger-Price-Adjustments-in-61-Pair-Study-1758194); [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/35703); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/tokenized-stocks-still-follow-wall-street-prices-study-by-exchange-group-finds/)
- CoinPaprika (Ondo case, 4 tickers, 1 weekend): weekend drift got the direction right every time, but magnitude was hard to predict. Tokens with an open redemption window drifted slightly more, not less. (Anecdotal: tiny sample.) — [CoinPaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/education/tokenized-stocks-price-tracking/)
- Other weekend reports: a "weekend gap of about 0.55%" figure (Anecdotal) — [Coincub](https://coincub.com/blog/24-7-tokenized-asset-trading/); tokenised AI stocks sliding over a weekend before the Monday reopen (Anecdotal) — [ETHNews](https://ethnews.com/tokenized-ai-stocks-slide-over-weekend-ahead-of-monday-reopen/)
- xStocks mechanics: Backed issues xStocks from a Liechtenstein SPV that buys or sells the underlying shares during US market hours after a mint or burn request. Primary-market arbitrage keeps tokens near the cash price only while Wall Street is open. (Strong evidence: Blockworks.) — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/tokenized-stocks-good-bad)
- Market makers cannot hedge a Sunday fill until Monday's open, so they quote wide spreads and small size. Wintermute's CEO said market makers are generally not keen to provide weekend liquidity. Kraken's own materials say spreads widen outside regular hours. (Strong evidence.) — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/tokenized-stocks-good-bad); [CoinPaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/education/tokenized-stocks-price-tracking/)
- Ondo mint/redeem: 24/5 at launch, tied to US market hours. 24/7 mint/redeem was added for the most-traded tokens (SPYon, QQQon, NVDAon, TSLAon, GOOGLon, CRCLon), reported from about 25 June / July 2026, with a $1 minimum and fees waived for **qualified purchasers / approved institutions**. Ondo says it uses "just-in-time" liquidity, minting and redeeming against Nasdaq/NYSE liquidity rather than building on-chain liquidity. Ondo has not disclosed how it settles off-chain when US markets are closed. (Strong evidence for the schedule; the liquidity description is company marketing.) — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/ondo-finance-247-tokenized-stocks-etfs/); [Bitget News](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605478831); [Ondo blog](https://ondo.finance/blog/what-sets-ondo-stocks-apart); [Ondo Stocks](https://ondo.finance/global-markets)
- Hyperliquid/trade.xyz equity perps follow share prices through oracle feeds, not tokenised shares. On weekends, when stock and futures exchanges are shut, these markets set prices with no outside reference market to copy. There are no exchange circuit breakers over a weekend gap. Weekend trading is quieter: trade.xyz was 11.6% of Hyperliquid perp volume on the median weekend day vs 25.5% on the median weekday, but still averaged about $541M a day on September weekends. (Strong evidence: 0xArchive analytics.) — [0xArchive](https://0xarchive.io/resources/market-data-guides/what-is-tradexyz); [Hyperliquid Guide](https://hyperliquidguide.com/ecosystem/hip-3-builder-codes)

**Collectibles lead-lag**
- Collector Crypt sources inventory at about 85% of market value through card shows, automated eBay bidding and bulk buys. Selling a high-value card through eBay or an auction house from the PSA Vault costs 7-16% in fees, vs 2% on Collector Crypt's marketplace. (Anecdotal: Datawallet.) — [Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/what-is-collector-crypt-cards)
- Buybacks: Courtyard's instant buyback is reported at about 90% of fair market value, paid in USDC; Blockworks measured an effective 84.6% quote and a 75.6% realised ratio. Collector Crypt's buyback runs about 85-93% of "insured value" depending on the machine, but this is not a guaranteed floor, and insured value is not an executable bid. (Strong evidence that buyback is 85-90% of the platform's own valuation; the exact numbers conflict.) — [Blockworks](https://app.blockworks.com/report/who-is-winning-the-onchain-gacha-trade); [PackRipper](https://www.packripper.net/blog/best-slab-pack-sites/); [bit.fan](https://www.bit.fan/en/news/list/collector-crypt-trading-card-infrastructure-cards-analysis-ba1b7abe-en-keyName-c975e97c); [PackSpy Courtyard](https://packspy.com/blog/how-does-courtyard-work)
- Realised gacha return-to-player is about 91.85-94.2% on Collector Crypt, and most wallets in a Bitquery study did not finish ahead in cash. (Anecdotal.) — [Kliment Dukovski](https://klimentdukovski.com/articles/collector-crypt-gacha-review/)

### Inferences
- In the Polymarket case, a liquid, continuously trading reference (Binance spot) existed and the venue repriced slowly. For tokenised stocks on a weekend, no liquid reference exists for single names. The real reference (NYSE/Nasdaq) is closed, and the only live proxies are CME index futures (indices only), other token venues, and trade.xyz perps, which are themselves "price-setting" over the weekend. So weekend deviation is largely gap risk that someone must warehouse, not a stale quote against a known true price. (Speculative but structurally strong.)
- A cleaner lag source may exist during **US market hours and pre-/post-market**: tokens that adjust to the underlying (WFE: "tokens make the larger adjustment") on thin DEX pools may lag a fast move in the underlying by seconds to minutes. This is closest to the 0x8dxd pattern, but it is also where professional arbitrageurs with mint/redeem access and CEX-hedged books operate. (Speculative.)
- Cross-venue token-vs-token gaps on weekends (e.g. an xStocks token on a Solana DEX vs Bybit/Kraken order book vs trade.xyz perp vs Ondo token) are a plausible small edge. It would be inventory-limited and needs non-UK access (section 5). (Speculative.)
- For collectibles, a lag strategy would be: buy tokenised cards listed below recent eBay/PSA sold comps (e.g. via 130point/PSA APR data), then sell into the platform buyback or marketplace, or redeem and sell on eBay. The 10-15% buyback haircut and 7-16% eBay fees mean the gap must exceed roughly 15-20% to profit, and comps are sparse and slow, with sale-to-settlement measured in days. (Speculative.)

### Gaps
- No reliable, independent, multi-month dataset of weekend token price vs Monday open by issuer/venue was found. The two main data points (92% vs 10%) conflict and neither methodology could be read.
- Whether trade.xyz uses an internal mark (e.g. an EMA of its own book) over weekends, and how it re-anchors at Monday open, was not confirmed from docs. (Unknown.)
- No measurement was found of oracle lag (Chainlink/RedStone/Pyth) for tokenised stock lending markets or perps during market hours. (Unknown.)
- No measured premium/discount to NAV series by issuer was found. (Unknown.)
- No study quantifies the gap between on-chain card prices and eBay/PSA sold prices. (Unknown.)

## 3. Liquidity, spreads, fees, depth; APIs

### Takeaway
Spot token liquidity is thin and spreads widen materially off-hours. Hyperliquid/trade.xyz is the deepest and most API-friendly venue, with currently tiny fees thanks to "growth mode". Collectible marketplaces have fees of about 2% (Collector Crypt) plus 10-15% haircuts on buybacks.

### Cited Findings
- Kraken says xStocks spreads widen outside regular hours, and market makers quote wide spreads and small size on weekends. (Strong evidence.) — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/tokenized-stocks-good-bad); [Kraken xStocks](https://www.kraken.com/xstocks)
- HIP-3 fees: the standard schedule is 2x Hyperliquid native perp fees, split 50/50 with the builder. "Growth mode" cuts this by 90%; Hyperliquid's API showed growth mode active on 105 of 110 markets on 3 Oct 2026. (Strong evidence: derived from the Hyperliquid API by a third party.) — [0xArchive](https://0xarchive.io/resources/market-data-guides/what-is-tradexyz); [Hyperliquid Guide](https://hyperliquidguide.com/ecosystem/hip-3-builder-codes)
- Single-name depth example: ORCL perp on trade.xyz showed about $3.55M in 24h volume in a May 2026 snapshot. Second-tier names are thin. (Anecdotal; stale.) — [Hyperliquid Guide ORCL](https://hyperliquidguide.com/markets/xyz/orcl)
- SpaceX token (SPCXx) episode, June 2026: demand outran supply. Bybit, Binance Wallet and Bitget Wallet cancelled campaigns and refunded users after the provider could not procure enough underlying shares. This is evidence that primary supply can bottleneck. (Strong evidence.) — [CryptoDaily](https://cryptodaily.co.uk/2026/06/solana-tokenized-stock-reality-check-spacex-xstocks-bottleneck); [Phemex](https://phemex.com/academy/tokenized-stocks-booming-on-solana-after-spacex-launch)
- Venues listing xStocks: Kraken, Bybit (spot, June 2025), Gate (spot and futures, July 2025), Solana DEXs, and Hyperliquid spot (Aug 2026). Bybit also runs dual-asset products on xStocks. (Verified/Strong evidence.) — [Bybit PR](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20823/release/20250630EN21098); [crypto.news Gate](https://crypto.news/gate-launches-xstocks-trading-section-bridging-crypto-finance-and-global-capital-markets/); [Payward](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-on-hyperliquid); [AAP/Cision Bybit](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20260723ae10987/)
- Collector Crypt marketplace fee is 2%, vs 7-16% for eBay or auction houses from the PSA Vault. (Anecdotal.) — [Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/what-is-collector-crypt-cards)

### Inferences
- API maturity: Hyperliquid (public REST/WebSocket, on-chain order book) and Solana DEXs (Jupiter/Raydium/Meteora, fully programmable) are the most bot-friendly. CEX venues (Bybit, Gate, Kraken) have standard APIs but impose KYC/jurisdiction checks. Courtyard and Collector Crypt are on-chain NFTs (Polygon/Solana) tradeable via marketplaces (e.g. Magic Eden, Tensor), so bots are technically feasible. (Speculative: not verified from API docs in this session.)
- Thin weekend books plus wide spreads mean the capacity of any weekend edge is small: likely thousands to tens of thousands of dollars of notional per event per name before the price moves against you. (Speculative.)

### Gaps
- No measured bid-ask spread or depth (e.g. ±1% depth) series by venue and hour was found for xStocks/Ondo tokens. (Unknown.) This needs to be pulled directly from Hyperliquid's API, Jupiter quotes, or Dune.
- Exact Kraken/Bybit/Gate fee schedules for tokenised stocks were not retrieved. (Unknown.)

## 4. Competition: market makers and arbitrage bots

### Takeaway
Professional liquidity is present in market hours (issuer-linked market makers, institutions with mint/redeem rights), and on Hyperliquid there is a deep crypto-native market-making ecosystem. Weekend liquidity is deliberately thin because pros avoid unhedgeable risk. That leaves room for a risk-taker, not a riskless lag-arb. There is no evidence of a lag that persists for long when a hedgeable reference exists.

### Cited Findings
- Primary-market arbitrage by authorised participants keeps xStocks near the cash price during US hours. (Strong evidence.) — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/tokenized-stocks-good-bad)
- Approved institutions can convert existing shares into Ondo tokens and back again. Retail cannot. (Verified: Ondo's own materials.) — [Ondo blog](https://ondo.finance/blog/what-sets-ondo-stocks-apart); [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/news/what-is-ondo-stocks)
- Wintermute's US arm registered as an SEC/FINRA broker-dealer and plans to move into tokenised equities. Its CEO says market makers are reluctant to provide weekend liquidity. (Strong evidence.) — [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/wintermute-gains-u-s-broker-status-eyes-tokenized-stocks/); [Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/wintermute-registers-as-us-broker-dealer-eyes-tokenized-stocks/); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/tokenized-stocks-good-bad)
- WFE: about 80% of 61 pairs are cointegrated with the underlying, which implies arbitrage forces exist but are imperfect. (Strong evidence.) — [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/35703)
- trade.xyz concentration: 98% of HIP-3 volume and open interest sits with one builder. (Strong evidence.) — [0xArchive](https://0xarchive.io/resources/market-data-guides/what-is-tradexyz)

### Inferences
- Polymarket's 15-minute crypto markets were briefly exploitable because the venue had few professional quoters and a perfectly hedgeable, ultra-liquid reference. Tokenised stocks in US hours have the hedgeable reference, but also institutional authorised participants and crypto market makers who can hedge with real shares, so gaps in US hours are likely closed in seconds. Off-hours there are fewer competitors but no hedgeable reference. The 0x8dxd-style "riskless stale quote" combination is therefore unlikely to exist at scale. (Speculative.)
- Collectibles markets have few sophisticated bots, as far as visible evidence goes. The platforms themselves (automated eBay bidding at about 85% of value, controlled buybacks) act as the dominant "market maker" and keep the spread to themselves. (Speculative, based on the Datawallet description.)

### Gaps
- No named market makers for xStocks, Ondo or Robinhood tokens on DEXs were found. (Unknown.)
- No data on how quickly cross-venue token gaps close (e.g. a half-life in seconds). (Unknown.) This would need on-chain trade analysis (Dune) or a live-recording experiment.

## 5. UK access and legality

### Takeaway
For a UK resident, most of this universe is closed or legally risky. xStocks are explicitly unavailable in the UK (Kraken and the xStocks site). Robinhood stock tokens are not offered in the UK. Ondo is closed to US persons, and its UK status was not confirmed. The FCA retail ban on crypto derivatives (in force since 6 Jan 2021) almost certainly captures equity perps settled in crypto on offshore venues for marketing and distribution purposes. Tokenised collectibles are accessible, but their pack/gacha mechanics sit in a grey zone under UK gambling law because prizes can be cashed out.

### Cited Findings
- Kraken lists xStocks among features unavailable in the United Kingdom. The xStocks FAQ says the product is available in 110+ countries excluding the US, UK, Canada and Australia. (Verified: Kraken support.) — [Kraken: where licensed](https://support.kraken.com/articles/where-is-kraken-licensed-or-regulated); [Kraken xStocks FAQ](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq); [Kraken xStocks](https://www.kraken.com/xstocks)

### Inferences
- **Tokenised stocks (xStocks, Robinhood, likely Ondo):** UK retail access is officially excluded. Using a VPN or offshore entity to bypass geo-blocks breaches venue terms (account freezes, forfeiture risk) and may engage UK securities and financial-promotion rules (FSMA s.21). This is a legal, not just a technical, risk. (Speculative but well-founded.)
- **Hyperliquid/trade.xyz equity perps:** these are derivatives referencing equities, settled in USDC. The 2021 FCA ban targets derivatives referencing *cryptoassets*, so equity-referencing perps may fall outside that specific ban but inside the general CFD/derivative regime, which requires an FCA-authorised provider. Hyperliquid is not FCA-authorised. Using it is not clearly a crime for the end user, but it is unregulated, with no recourse, and front-ends may geo-block the UK. (Speculative; needs legal advice.)
- **Tokenised collectibles:** Buying and selling already-minted card NFTs on a secondary market is ordinary trading of a physical-backed collectible. Pack/gacha purchases with cash-out buybacks look closer to gambling under the UK cash-out test. A bot that only trades secondary listings and buybacks (not pack opening) is legally cleaner. (Speculative.)
- A UK limited company buying from offshore venues does not obviously cure the problem, because venue exclusions are typically by residence or incorporation. (Speculative.)

## 7. Realistic profit capacity for a small UK operator, and scoring

### Takeaway
Tokenised stocks fit the "slow, retail, growing" brief but fail the two conditions that made 0x8dxd work: a riskless, liquid live reference during the lag window, and legal access for this founder. Tokenised collectibles are accessible and slow, but the "edge" is closer to card-flipping with 15-20%+ round-trip friction and low capacity: plausibly low thousands of dollars a month, unproven.
